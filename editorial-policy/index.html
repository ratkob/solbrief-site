How SolBrief is written

Published by: Ratko Božović, Rincón de la Victoria, Málaga, Spain · solbrief@ratko.biz

Written by AI, from named sources

SolBrief has no human editor. Stories and the audio edition are written and voiced by AI from official sources (town councils, AEMET, DGT, BOJA) and local press. Every story carries the label "Written by AI from official sources and local press" and lists and links every source it used.

Checks before anything is published

  • A big story needs two independent outlets or one official source.
  • Every number, date, time and place must appear in a source. If sources give different figures, dates or times for the same thing, the story says so and names each one, the official source's first. If that cannot be settled, the figure is left out.
  • Every sentence must be backed by a passage in a source.
  • A second AI model, never the one that wrote the story, checks each claim.
  • No legal, tax or medical advice, no "safe to swim", no invented deadlines, and a proposal is never written as a decision.
  • Facts only, in our own words. No copied text, no outside photographs. Every outlet is credited and linked.
  • A story must be recent and about the right town.

If a story cannot pass these checks it is dropped. A short edition is better than a wrong one.

Corrections

Published stories are re-checked every two hours and updated or withdrawn when the facts change. Tap Report a mistake on any story to trigger an immediate re-check. Corrections are shown on the story.

Illustrations

Illustrations are original AI-generated paintings made for SolBrief. They are scenes, not photographs of the event described.

No advertising

There are no ads or sponsored listings in SolBrief.
