SolBrief Support

  • Email: solbrief@ratko.biz
  • Restore purchases: open SolBrief → Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription: open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → SolBrief, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Report a mistake in a story: tap Report a mistake at the end of the story. The story is re-checked straight away.
  • Privacy choices: open SolBrief → Settings → Privacy.
