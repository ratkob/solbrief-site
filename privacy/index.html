SolBrief Privacy Policy (EU GDPR & Spanish AEPD)

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 · Controller: Ratko Bozovic (Rincon de la Victoria, Malaga, Spain · support@ratko.biz)

1. On-Device Household Profile (Zero Cloud Profile Storage)

Your selected home municipality, secondary towns, children's age brackets, housing status, and car ownership preference are stored strictly on your iPhone in local storage (MMKV). They are never transmitted to any third-party AI provider.

2. Zero GPS Location Tracking

SolBrief never requests iOS CoreLocation or GPS permissions. Drive-time zones (Zone A ≤ 35 min, Zone B 36-150 min) are computed from a static municipality matrix based on the town you select.

3. Dual-Path AI & Spanish PII Redaction (Explain This Letter)

When you scan a Spanish administrative document:

  • Apple Intelligence Devices (iPhone 15 Pro+ / iOS 26+): OCR and document explanation run 100% on-device using Apple Vision and Apple Foundation Models.
  • Non-Apple-Intelligence Devices: On-device Apple Vision OCR extracts text and a deterministic on-device regex scrubber redacts Spanish NIE, DNI, CIF, IBAN, phone numbers, and email addresses before requesting your explicit one-time consent to send the redacted text to our Supabase Edge Function. Raw document images never leave your device.

4. Two-Tier GDPR & ePrivacy Analytics Posture

Prior to consent (or if you choose Decline (Essential Only)), PostHog EU runs in cookieless memory-only mode and Firebase Analytics and Singular MMP remain disabled. Only if you tap Accept Analytics do we enable persistent product analytics and request iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.
